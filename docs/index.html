World Shift & Global News

World Shift (English), World Shift Deutsch (German) and Global News (Turkish) publish short, independent news analysis videos about geopolitics, technology and the global economy.

Our internal publishing tool prepares videos, sends each one to the channel owner for manual review and approval, and only then publishes approved videos to the channel owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok).
